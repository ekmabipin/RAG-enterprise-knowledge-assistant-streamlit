NovaTech Solutions
Company Frequently Asked Questions

Document Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document Owner: Human Resources

Q1. How many annual leave days do permanent full-time employees receive?
A. Permanent full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Detailed rules are available in the NovaTech Solutions Leave Policy.

Q2. Can unused annual leave be carried forward?
A. Yes. Carry-forward is allowed subject to the limits and expiration rules defined in the NovaTech Solutions Leave Policy.

Q3. Who should I contact for questions about my leave balance?
A. Employees should contact Human Resources for questions about leave balances or leave-policy interpretation.

Q4. Can I work remotely?
A. Remote work may be available depending on the employee's role, business requirements, performance expectations, manager approval, and information-security requirements.

Q5. Is remote work automatically available to every employee?
A. No. Remote work is not an automatic entitlement and requires approval.

Q6. Is there any home-internet reimbursement for remote employees?
A. Eligible employees may claim approved home-internet expenses under the NovaTech Solutions Remote Work Policy.

Q7. Can I use my personal email account to send company documents?
A. No. Confidential company information must not be sent to personal email accounts or stored on unauthorized systems.

Q8. What should I do if I lose my company laptop?
A. Report the lost or stolen device immediately to IT and your manager.

Q9. Can I install software on my company laptop?
A. Employees must not install unauthorized software. Business software should be obtained through company-approved channels.

Q10. Do I need to use VPN when working remotely?
A. Employees must use company-approved remote-access methods, and VPN must be used where required by the relevant system or the Information Security team.

Q11. What class of air travel should I normally book?
A. Employees should normally book economy class. Other travel classes require approval under the Travel and Expense Policy.

Q12. How soon should I submit travel expenses?
A. Travel expenses should normally be submitted within 15 calendar days after completion of the business trip.

Q13. Are personal entertainment expenses reimbursable?
A. No. Personal entertainment is generally not reimbursable.

Q14. Does NovaTech provide employee medical insurance?
A. Eligible employees are covered under the company-sponsored group medical insurance plan, subject to the applicable plan terms.

Q15. Is there a wellness benefit?
A. Yes. Eligible employees may claim approved wellness-related expenses under the Employee Benefits Guide.

Q16. Can unused wellness benefit amounts be carried forward?
A. No. Unused wellness benefit amounts cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.

Q17. What should I do if I receive a suspicious authentication request?
A. Do not approve an unexpected authentication request. Suspicious or repeated authentication prompts should be reported to IT.

Q18. Can I share my company password with a teammate?
A. No. Employees must not share passwords, authentication codes, or other access credentials.

Q19. What should I do if I observe harassment or bullying?
A. Employees should raise concerns through their manager, Human Resources, Compliance, or another available company reporting channel.

Q20. Is retaliation allowed against an employee who reports a concern?
A. No. NovaTech Solutions prohibits retaliation against employees who raise concerns in good faith.

Q21. Can I work remotely from another country?
A. Not without prior approval. Employees must obtain approval from Human Resources, their manager, and any other required company function.

Q22. Can I submit personal sightseeing costs as a business travel expense?
A. No. Personal sightseeing expenses are not reimbursable.

Q23. Are public holidays deducted from annual leave?
A. No. Company-designated public holidays are separate from annual leave entitlement.

Q24. Can sick leave be carried forward?
A. No. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.

Q25. Where should I go for detailed policy information?
A. Employees should refer to the relevant dedicated policy document, such as the Leave Policy, Remote Work Policy, IT Security Policy, Travel and Expense Policy, Employee Benefits Guide, or Code of Conduct.
